One account for Chrome and Code.

Connect Chrome once. After that, choose an email in the menu bar and carry on where you were.

1. Add the Chrome companion

  1. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome.
  2. Turn on Developer mode, then choose Load unpacked.
  3. Select this folder:
    ~/Library/Application Support/Claude Switcher/BrowserExtension
    In the folder picker, press ⇧⌘G and paste that path.

The companion reads and switches Claude.ai cookies only. Saved sessions stay in your Mac’s Keychain. It does not access Gmail or other websites.

2. Save each account once

  1. Sign in at Claude.ai.
  2. The companion saves configured accounts automatically. Open Claude Switcher Companion to see the detected email; Save this Claude login also saves it on demand.
  3. Choose Add another account in the companion and sign in to the next account. Repeat once per account. Use this button instead of Claude’s Sign out so the saved session is not revoked.

Then just choose an email.

Chrome changes to that account. Your next claude, claude --continue, or claude --resume uses it too, in Ghostty or iTerm2. Running sessions stay open; stop and resume them when ready.

A single Chrome profile has one active Claude account. All Claude.ai tabs in that profile share the switch. A revoked login needs to be saved again. Claude Desktop has its own separate sign-in.
